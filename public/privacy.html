Build Spotter

Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026 · Updated November 22, 2026 to add Google Analytics

Build Spotter is a web app that identifies cars and car parts from photos and helps people plan modifications for their own car with an AI build guide. It is run by Joshua George, an independent developer in Walpole, Massachusetts, USA ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it. It applies to the app at this website and every page on it.

2. Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to understand how people use Build Spotter: how many people visit, which pages and features they use, what kind of device and browser they have, and roughly where they are (country or city, worked out from the IP address). Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to tell visits apart. It does not receive your photos, your Creative mode messages, your email address or your password, and we do not link analytics data to your account.

We use this information only to improve the app. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy; you can read how Google uses data from sites that use its services. To opt out, install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or block or clear cookies in your browser settings. The app works the same without them.

3. Signing in with Google

You can create an account or sign in with your Google account. When you do, Google shares the following with us, and only after you agree on Google's consent screen:

  • Your email address (the email scope)
  • Your name and profile picture (the profile scope)
  • A Google account ID that lets us recognise you next time (the openid scope)

We do not ask for, and cannot see, anything else in your Google account: not your Gmail, contacts, Drive, Calendar, Photos or password.

How we use Google user data

We use your Google email, name and profile picture only to create and sign in to your Build Spotter account, to keep your scan balance linked to you, and to send account emails. We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not use it to train AI models.

How we store and share Google user data

Your Google email, name, profile picture and account ID are stored in our account database, run by Supabase, and are not shared with anyone else except as required by law. They are never sent to our AI provider, to Meshy, or to Google Analytics.

Build Spotter's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing Google access

You can disconnect Build Spotter from your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. To also delete the data we hold, see Deleting your data.

4. How we use your information

  • To run the app: identify the cars and parts in your photos, reply in Creative mode, and make 3D models when you ask for them.
  • To manage your account, scan balance and purchases, including refunding scans that fail.
  • To send account emails (sign-up confirmation, password reset). We don't send marketing emails.
  • To prevent abuse and keep the service working, and to meet legal obligations.

5. Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run Build Spotter. Each receives only what it needs for its job:

  • Supabase: stores accounts (including Google sign-in details), scan balances and 3D garage files.
  • Netlify: hosts the website and the server functions that handle scans.
  • An AI provider (Anthropic): receives each photo you scan and your Creative mode messages, so it can identify parts and reply. It does not receive your email, name or Google data.
  • Meshy: only if you use the 3D garage. It receives the car photos you choose and your car's details to draw your car and build 3D models, and keeps its copies for up to 3 days.
  • Stripe: processes payments for scan packs.
  • Google Analytics: receives usage data about visits (see Analytics and cookies). It does not receive your Google sign-in details, photos or messages.

We do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or give it to data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. How long we keep it

  • Photos and Creative mode messages: not stored by us. They are processed and then discarded.
  • Account details (email, name, profile picture, scan balance and history): kept while your account exists, and deleted within 30 days of your request.
  • 3D garage drawings and models: kept while your account exists so you can reopen them. Anyone with a model's direct link can view it. Deleted on request.
  • Payment records: kept as long as required for tax and accounting.
  • Analytics data: kept by Google Analytics for 14 months, then deleted automatically.

7. Deleting your data

Email joshua.math.george@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will delete your account, your Google sign-in details, your scan history and your 3D garage files within 30 days and confirm by email. Unused bought scans are lost when an account is deleted. Builds and recent scans saved in your browser are removed by clearing your browser's site data.

8. Security

All traffic to Build Spotter uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored hashed, the server keys that can read account data are kept only on the server, and card details never reach us. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

9. Children

Build Spotter is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how we use Google user data, we will ask for your consent again before using it in the new way.
