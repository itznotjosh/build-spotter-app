Build Spotter

Privacy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Build Spotter identifies cars and car parts from photos you choose to scan. This page says what happens to your information when you use it.

Your photos

When you scan a photo, it is sent to our server and passed to an AI service that identifies the car and its parts. We do not keep your photos. Your recent scans are saved in your browser, on your device only, so you can open them again. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Creative mode

When you plan a build, the car details you enter and the messages you send to the build guide go to our server and on to the same AI service, so it can reply. We do not keep your conversations. Your builds are saved in your browser, on your device only. We keep a count of how many guide replies each build session has used, so we know when a session's scan is used up.

3D garage

If you turn on the 3D garage for a build, we send Meshy (a 3D modelling service) the photos of your car you choose, if any, and your car's details and the parts you add, so it can draw your car and make 3D models of it. To describe each version accurately, your build guide conversation is also passed to the same AI service that runs the guide (not to Meshy). Meshy keeps its copies for up to 3 days. We don't keep your photos. We keep the drawings and 3D models of each version in our storage, linked to your account, so you can open them again and so new versions can be drawn from earlier ones. Anyone with a model's link can view it. Email us to have them deleted.

Your account

To scan, you make an account with an email address and a password. We store your email, an encrypted form of your password, and how many scans you have used and bought. We use your email to sign you in and to send account messages such as password resets.

Payments

Scan packs are bought through Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never reach us. We receive a record that the payment succeeded so we can add your scans.

Who else handles your information

  • Supabase stores accounts and scan balances.
  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Netlify hosts the app.
  • Meshy makes 3D models from the photo or description you send it; Supabase stores the finished models.
  • An AI provider receives each photo you scan in order to identify it, and your Creative mode messages so the build guide can reply.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Deleting your account

Email us from the address on your account and we will delete the account and its scan history.
